Senior Backend Engineer — Job Description

We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to help design and scale our core platform services.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python
- Hands-on experience with Docker and container orchestration (Kubernetes)
- Experience designing and building REST APIs
- Experience with AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda, or similar)
- Solid SQL and relational database experience
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines

Nice to have:
- Experience with GraphQL
- Experience mentoring junior engineers
- Exposure to microservices architecture

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain backend services powering our product
- Collaborate with frontend and product teams on API contracts
- Improve system reliability, performance, and observability
- Participate in code reviews and mentor other engineers
